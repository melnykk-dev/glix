# Third-Party Licenses — Glix

Date: 2026-11-12. Owner: Licenses Lead (Studio Gate item 8).

**Rule: every third-party asset that ships with Glix is listed here, with its
source, its license, and its SHA-256. No asset ships without a line in this
file.** Art and audio are CC0 1.0 only. The one font is SIL Open Font
License 1.1. Attribution is not required for any of these, but credit is
given voluntarily (see `packages/editor/src/templates/assets/CREDITS.md`).

Verification method: each Kenney pack's `License.txt` (inside the downloaded
zip) was read directly on 2026-11-12 — all eight state CC0 1.0. Pack
source pages were also verified earlier (`office/contracts/ASSET_LICENSES.md`).
Every file-backed asset below was hash-checked: the bytes embedded in the
templates decode to exactly the SHA-256 in
`packages/editor/src/templates/assets/MANIFEST.json`.
